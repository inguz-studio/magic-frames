About a year and a half ago, I set two very ambitious goals to hit $10,000 a month and hit
100,000 followers on Instagram. These two goals were literally everything to me. At the time,
I had like 2,000 followers and I was making less than $1,000 a month. Fast forward a year and a
half now, I'm a sophomore in college, I've hit my fourth 10k month, I'm very close to 100,000
followers, like I'm going to hit it probably in the next week. And I wanted to take today to reflect
on two really weird things that I learned since then. First one is that the brain is most happy
when it is in the state of chasing a goal, not after it has succeeded in it. Like I thought
things would magically change after I hit it. My mind is just focused on other problems. I am just
posting every single day and then the next day I wake up and I'm just focusing on what I'm going
to post for the next day. How are we going to maintain the growth? What is my housing situation
going to look like next year? I wish I could spend more time with certain friends. I guess this leads
to my second point about money specifically, which is that $10,000 a month does not feel like a lot and
I still feel pretty broke. I'm putting aside 30% 30% to taxes and then I'm saving like half of what's
left after that. So that's like 3.5k. I don't know, I had this image, maybe it was after watching
these YouTube videos of these Iman Godzi flying around in private jets and shit. Like my life would
look really different, but I get to just eat out a little bit more. I guess I can spend money on like
a trip or two every few months. But yeah, nothing really changes. Enjoy the process while you're doing
it. I'll see you guys tomorrow for day 57. Peace.
