I ran an experiment to test if it's true that that chlorine scent you smell from a pool
is really just from pee. So I got two identical buckets and filled them both with pure water.
Then I added four times the recommended concentration of chlorine for that volume
of water to both buckets. And then finally, as the only point of difference, I added just a
little bit of pee to bucket B, and then I let him sit for a day. And so then it was time for
the smell test. First bucket A, that was just chlorine and water. Hmm. So that doesn't smell
like anything. That smells just like water, even though this has four times the recommended
concentration of chlorine for this volume. And then it was bucket B's turn. What? This smells
like a pool. This is a nostalgic smell. This smells like summers and vacationing at hotels
with pools and water parks. Turns out it was just pee.
