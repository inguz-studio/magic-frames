Which brand actually has the better lifetime warranty?
Patagonia or Osprey?
They both promise to stand behind their gear, but not in the same way.
Patagonia's ironclad guarantee follows the four Rs.
You can repair, replace, return, or refund your purchase.
But here's the part most people miss.
Repairs from normal wear and tear can come with a cost,
and people say the process can be slow or expensive.
Osprey, on the other hand, takes a very different approach.
Their almighty guarantee covers repairs or replacements for absolutely any reason.
One Reddit user sent in a bag that looked like it had been chewed on by a dog,
and received a brand new Osprey bag in return.
This just shows how far Osprey is willing to go.
