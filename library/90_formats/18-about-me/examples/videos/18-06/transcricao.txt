Here are my top three premium tech items.
First up is the Nintendo Switch 2.
I picked this up about two months ago, and it's easily one of the most fun devices I have used this year.
I wanted something portable with solid performance so I can play on a plane or in bed, and this thing did not disappoint.
The games are definitely more expensive compared to the other handhelds, but hey, you can't have everything.
Next up is the Even Realities G2.
This is one of those products that feels like the future the moment you put it on.
Clean design, super lightweight, and packed with smart features you can actually use on a daily basis.
From real-time translation into subtle notifications, it feels less like tech and more like a natural extension.
Subtle, useful, and seriously impressive.
And last but not least, the Rayneo Air 3S Pro.
This is honestly my go-to device for long travels.
Imagine having a 200-inch screen in front of you while sitting on a 10-hour flight.
It completely changes how you watch, play, or get work done on the go.
This thing goes with me every time I travel.
