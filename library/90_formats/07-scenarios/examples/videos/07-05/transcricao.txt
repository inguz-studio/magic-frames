If you swapped brains with your mom, would you be you inside your mom's body?
Well, doctors would have to remove both brains without damaging them.
Then each one would need to be placed into the other person's skull.
Now your brain connects to your body through millions of tiny nerves in your spinal cord.
So to make the swap work, every single nerve would need to be reconnected perfectly
so you could move, feel, and think like normal.
But even if it were possible, some memories and personality are tied to your own physical body,
meaning something would feel off and you might never fully be yourself again.
